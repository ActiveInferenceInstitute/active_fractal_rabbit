host is closed with tests; the manuscript reports falsification-style negative controls and
holdout scoring from hydrated variables; the rendered PDF passes the project's own audit;
the claim ledger covers the new claims.

- Publishing/releasing; committing unrelated co-actor changes in the dirty sidecar repo.
