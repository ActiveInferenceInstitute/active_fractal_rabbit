## Current Evidence

The scholarship ledger and challenge-pack literature refresh map primary-source
mobility surveys, foundation and LLM mobility candidates, synthetic-trip
utility evaluation, higher-order mobility-network evaluation, privacy-risk
sources, and reproducibility sources to allowed wording, forbidden overreach,
official or DOI/arXiv source status, and manuscript surfaces.

The 2026-07-07 ULW review directly verified and admitted a narrow set of
additional sources: R-AIF/world-model active inference, Active Inference Tree
Search, a formal-equivalence review, self-supervised disentanglement mobility
prediction, sparse trajectory prediction, and the Nature Communications
universal mobility model. They are ledger-bound as future comparators or formal
context only; none is current benchmark evidence.

## Blocked And Unblocked Conditions

Remaining work is leaderboard admission for auditable locked-split adapters.
Candidate rows, including the newly admitted literature candidates, stay blocked
until adapter, license, compute, data, dependency, and locked-split contracts are
auditable.

Future research-refresh passes must triage Perplexity or web-scout output into
three buckets before manuscript admission: primary-source candidates, already
covered sources, and non-admissible commentary. Blogs, forums, talks, and
general knowledge-base pages can inform review questions but cannot become
source-of-record rows for manuscript claims unless they are explicitly scoped as
non-peer-reviewed exposition and no stronger source is available.

## Implementation Scope

Maintain `docs/scholarship_ledger.md`, `manuscript/references.bib`, challenge
pack literature candidates, and docs-contract coverage. Add only DOI, arXiv,
official publisher, or official project sources.

When active-inference objective sources are refreshed, keep canonical EFE,
FEEF/objective-debate, `pymdp` diagnostic, and local proxy claims separate.
Literature can sharpen the formal distinction, but it cannot upgrade a proxy
score, VFE surface, or EFE decomposition into a predictive-performance claim.

## Non-Goals

Do not treat literature-map inclusion as evidence that a method clears the
local benchmark. Do not cite unofficial source mirrors when a primary source is
available.

## Acceptance Gates

Docs-contract scholarship tests pass, every manuscript citation key has an
allowed/forbidden ledger row, and admitted leaderboard rows meet locked-split
and dependency-audit requirements.

## Artifact And Provenance Surfaces

Primary surfaces include `docs/scholarship_ledger.md`,
`manuscript/references.bib`, `open_challenge_pack.json`,
`sota_program_leaderboard.csv`, claim-ledger rows, and rendered manuscript
citations.

## Claim Boundary

Literature context can motivate a baseline or limitation. It cannot turn a
candidate into global SOTA or empirical validation without the generated gate.
