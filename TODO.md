## Standing Claim Locks

Synthetic SOTA progress cannot unlock national-security deployment,
surveillance-readiness, operational tasking, real-mobility validity, empirical
transfer, or global SOTA language. Local AIF leads must cite the exact
regenerated synthetic regime, fair baseline, uncertainty, multiplicity status,
and artifact gate.

EFE/VFE surfaces remain audit-only until predictive gates clear. Policy
posterior, epistemic value, pragmatic value, EFE, and VFE can improve
interpretability, but they cannot substitute for next-cell predictive
improvement or imply collection recommendations.

No baseline is weakened to manufacture a win. A future positive must beat the
strongest implemented fair baseline under the locked split, with calibration,
runtime, minimization, integrity, leak checks, lane coverage, and uncertainty
all visible.
