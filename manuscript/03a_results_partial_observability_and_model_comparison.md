## Noisy Emissions Reward Belief Marginalization, Not Point Estimates {#sec:results-model-comparison-fixture}

### Soft Marginalization Beats Point Estimation as Emissions Degrade {#sec:results-partial-observability}

Under a known noisy emission, uncertainty-preserving belief marginalization lowers predictive loss by {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_NOISE_ADVANTAGE}} nats on the primary fixture draw. This is the benchmark's one replicated positive mechanism-level regime.

Active inference realizes that mechanism through recursive hidden-state belief updating in a discrete POMDP generative model [@dacosta2020discrete_active_inference]. HMM, particle, state-space, and soft-filter methods can implement the same belief-preserving mechanism when they propagate a calibrated posterior over the hidden state [@kaelbling1998pomdp; @rabiner1989hmm; @arulampalam2002particle_filters; @doucet2001smc; @patterson2008state_space_movement; @patterson2017movement_models]. Their performance can still diverge through model misspecification, approximate inference, finite-sample estimation, adaptation, and policy structure [@dacosta2020discrete_active_inference; @arulampalam2002particle_filters; @patterson2017movement_models]. When the observation directly names the discretized latent cell, the posterior is already concentrated and a point estimate supplies the same information.

The protocol of [@sec:formal-partial-observability] isolates the condition that rewards recursive belief: a latent state, a noisy emission, and prediction of the true latent next cell. Soft (marginalize the full belief), hard (commit to the MAP cell), and observed-Markov (commit to the raw noisy observation) share the same learned transition, emission, observations, and priors. Marginalize-versus-commit is the sole treatment difference, eliminating the asymmetry that invalidated the earlier noise frontier ([@sec:roadmap-aif-deepening]). The log score rewards the full predictive distribution [@gneiting2007scoring], so this lane tests hidden-state filtering and probabilistic prediction. It does not isolate an expected-free-energy policy-selection advantage [@millidge2020efe].

Across {{PARTIAL_OBS_N_CELLS}} latent cells, the soft belief advantage over the point estimate is {{PARTIAL_OBS_CLEAN_ADVANTAGE}} nats at zero noise (zero-at-clean: {{PARTIAL_OBS_ZERO_WHEN_CLEAN}}) and rises monotonically with the observation-noise level (monotone: {{PARTIAL_OBS_MONOTONE}}), reaching {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_NOISE_ADVANTAGE}} ± {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_NOISE_SD}} nats at ε = {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_EPS}} ([@fig:partial-observability]). The oracle-fed reference remains the predictive ceiling; the measured gain belongs to the declared synthetic emission contract.

The growth is close to linear in the flip probability: an ordinary-least-squares fit of the mean advantage against ε gives a slope of {{PARTIAL_OBS_NOISE_SLOPE}} nats per unit ε with R² = {{PARTIAL_OBS_NOISE_R2}}. The near-linearity has a mechanistic reading. Flips are independent across steps, and each flip costs a committing predictor a roughly fixed penalty: it propagates the wrong row of the transition. The soft belief retains $(1-\varepsilon)$ of its mass on the true cell, so to first order the excess loss scales with the flip rate. On this reading the slope estimates the average per-flip price of committing.

The spread is a robustness band, not a generalization interval: each level aggregates {{PARTIAL_OBS_N_REALIZATIONS}} paired soft-versus-hard resamplings (noise draws × locked holdout splits), and *every* resampling at *every* nonzero noise level is positive. The worst single resampling at the strongest noise still favors the soft belief filter by {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_NOISE_MIN}} nats; the clean channel carries no advantage by construction, as the zero-at-clean row above records.

The between-draw check repeats the construction across {{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_COUNT}} independent synthetic generator draws with {{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_TRAVELLERS}} travellers each. The noisy-emission sign remains stable ({{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_SIGN_STABLE}}), and at ε = {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_EPS}} the replicated advantage is smaller than the headline: the cross-draw mean is {{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_MEAN_ADVANTAGE}} nats (range {{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_MIN_ADVANTAGE}}–{{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_MAX_ADVANTAGE}} nats across draws, largest between-draw SD {{PARTIAL_OBS_CROSS_DRAW_MAX_SD}} nats). The {{PARTIAL_OBS_MAX_NOISE_ADVANTAGE}}-nat headline is therefore the primary {{TRAVELLER_COUNT}}-traveller fixture-draw value, not the replicated magnitude; the replication establishes the sign and a more conservative effect size.

The advantage varies in magnitude and remains unanimous in sign, while the broader benchmark gate stays `{{PYMDP_SOTA_STATUS}}`. The fair-comparison ladder and traveller×noise power map locate the active-inference row within this mechanism in the Discussion ([@sec:discussion]).
